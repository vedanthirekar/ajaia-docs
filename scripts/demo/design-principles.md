# Design Principles

These principles guide every product decision on the team.

## 1. Calm by default

The page is the product. Formatting stays out of the way until you need it.

## 2. Safe to share

- Sharing is **explicit**: owners choose who can view and who can edit.
- Nothing is overwritten silently. If two people save at once, the second person is told.
- Public links are *read-only*, always.

## 3. Fast to start

1. Click **New document**, or import a file you already have.
2. Start typing. Markdown shortcuts work as you go.
3. Share when you are ready.

> Good tools disappear. People should remember their work, not the editor.
